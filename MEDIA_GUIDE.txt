=====================================================================
   HOW TO CHANGE THE PHOTOS AND VIDEOS ON THE WEBSITE
   Caribbean Incentive · simple guide, no technical knowledge needed
=====================================================================

With this guide you received two folders:

   img     all the photos on the website
   video   the moving videos at the top of the home page

The idea is simple:

   Every photo has a fixed name and a fixed place.
   To change a photo, put your new photo in the SAME FOLDER
   with the EXACT SAME NAME, so it replaces the old one.
   Then email that folder back to your developer, who puts it live.

You don't need any special software, only your computer and email.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 · REPLACE A PHOTO  (on your computer, about 5 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1. Unzip the folders you received.
        Right-click the zip file > "Extract All" > "Extract".

STEP 2. Find the photo you want to change.
        Look it up in PART 3 below. It tells you which folders to open,
        for example:  img > islands > anguilla > hero.jpg
        Tip: inside a folder, click "View" > "Large icons" to see the
        pictures, so you can check it's the right one.

STEP 3. Make sure you can see the end of file names.
        Open File Explorer > click "View" at the top > "Show" >
        tick "File name extensions".
        Now you see names like  hero.jpg  instead of just  hero

STEP 4. Make sure your new photo is the same TYPE.
        Look at the last letters of the name:
           .jpg  almost all photos
           .png  only the logos and the team photo
           .mp4  only the home page videos
        If your photo is another type (for example .jpeg, .heic or .png
        from a phone), convert it:
           Open it in Paint > File > Save as > "JPEG picture".

STEP 5. Keep it light, so the website stays fast.
        Photos should be under 400 KB, videos under 10 MB.
        (Right-click the photo > Properties shows its size.)
        If a photo is too big: open it in Paint > "Resize" >
        choose "Pixels" > type 1400 in the width box > OK > Save.

        Use the same SHAPE as the old photo:
           WIDE ....... like a postcard lying flat (most photos)
           TALL ....... like a phone standing up (the island "card" photos)
           SQUARE ..... the team photo only
        The website trims the edges to fit, so keep the important part
        (people, the view) in the MIDDLE of the photo.

STEP 6. Rename your new photo to the EXACT same name as the old one.
        Right-click your photo > Rename > type the name, e.g.  hero.jpg
        Small letters, same spelling, same dashes, no spaces.
           Correct:   the-island.jpg
           Wrong:     The Island.jpg     the_island.jpg     the-island (1).jpg

STEP 7. Drag your renamed photo into the folder from STEP 2.
        Windows asks "Replace or Skip Files".
        Click "Replace the file in the destination".
        Done: the old photo is replaced.

Repeat STEP 2 to STEP 7 for every photo you want to change.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 · SEND IT BACK  (by email)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Send back the FOLDER that holds your new photo, not the photo alone.
(Many photos have the same name, for example every island has a
hero.jpg, so the folder tells us which island it belongs to.)

STEP 1. Right-click that folder (for example the "anguilla" folder) >
        "Send to" > "Compressed (zipped) folder".
        A new file appears:  anguilla.zip

STEP 2. Attach the zip file to an email to your developer.
        In the email, write one line per change, for example:
           "New Anguilla top photo"
           "New photo for the Welcome section on the home page"

STEP 3. Changed photos in several folders? Zip each folder and attach
        them all. If the email says the files are too big (more than
        about 20 MB), send them in two or three emails, or share them
        with a OneDrive or Google Drive link instead.

Your developer puts them on the website. You'll see them within a day.

PART 4 · THE BROCHURE (PDF)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The "Brochure" link on Our Story and Let's Connect opens your PDF,
and the chat assistant reads it to answer visitors' questions.
To change it, simply email the new PDF to your developer.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK ANSWERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

I'm not sure which photo it is
   > Open the folder with "View" > "Large icons" and look at the
     pictures. Or send your developer a screenshot of the page and
     your new photo, and they will place it.

I replaced the wrong photo
   > No problem. Nothing changes on the website until your developer
     uploads it, and every old photo is kept. Just tell them.

My photo looks cut off on the website
   > The website trims edges to fit. Use a photo where the important
     part is in the middle, in the same shape as the old one.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
FOR THE DEVELOPER (the client can skip this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The shared folders are static/img and static/video. Returned folders go
back to the same place under static/, then commit and push; Vercel
redeploys. Paths live in content/media.py (templates never hard-code
them). Static files are cached by browsers for 7 days (vercel.json), so
a replaced file under the same name can show the old version to returning
visitors; rename the file and update content/media.py if it must change
for everyone at once. Any static/video/hero*.mp4 is picked up for the
home hero automatically. A new brochure goes in both static/docs/
(caribbean-incentive-brochure.pdf) and knowledge/ (for the assistant).
Before uploading, check each photo is under ~400 KB and each video
under ~10 MB.
